The Best Shows According to Siam

My top 3 movies of all-time

Cobra Kai

I am learning how to practice martial arts. I am doing kickboxing, MuayThai and all that. Watching Cobra Kai inspired me to try something that can help me defend my self. Practicing a physical skill like this helps me find balance and it removes the void of trying to feel inferior. The values the cobra kai senseis teach are real world explanations even if its fiction.

The Rookie

Watching the main character John Nolan navigate his life as a police Rookie at age 40+ made me like his character when everyone looked down on him but his maturity and humour helped me appreciate the police officer. Future seasons he's not as interesting but the show ain't half bad.

Wednesday Season: 1

I am a fan of the girl who plays Enid. Even though Wednesday was the protagonist I just liked the dynamic between her and Enid. Enid is also very cheerful character who is an outcast in a way but Season 1 version was really great.
